# 2021-Data-Analysis-Fellowship-Capstone
**Progressive Democrats of New Jersey | Data Analysis Fellowship**

**R | Power BI | Data Cleaning | Data Visualization | Exploratory Data Analysis | Dashboard Development**

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the **2021 Data Analysis Fellowship with Progressive Democrats of New Jersey (PDNJ)**. The project analyzed New Jersey demographic and socioeconomic data to examine population characteristics, racial demographics, household income, poverty, education, and employment.

The analysis used **R for data preparation and cleaning** and **Power BI to create an interactive dashboard and communicate findings**.

## Project Objective
The project's purpose was to help identify **important demographic characteristics and socioeconomic issues affecting people in New Jersey**.

The analysis focused on:
-	Population by age 
-	Racial demographics 
-	County-level demographic differences 
-	Household income by race 
-	Poverty by race 
-	Education levels 
-	Employment levels and growth by industry 

## Data Preparation
I used R to prepare the datasets for analysis and visualization.
The data preparation process included:
-	Importing multiple CSV datasets 
-	Examining data structure and data types 
-	Checking for missing values 
-	Removing unnecessary ID and geography fields 
-	Renaming variables for consistency 
-	Converting percentage/share values into percentage formats 
-	Creating processed CSV files for use in the analysis 

The project included statewide data as well as demographic data for **Union, Essex, and Middlesex counties**.

## Data Analysis & Visualization
Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard containing nine areas of analysis:
1.	**Age of Both Sexes — 2010–2019** 
2.	**Race by New Jersey — 2013–2019** 
3.	**Race by Union County — 2013–2019** 
4.	**Race by Essex County — 2013–2019** 
5.	**Race by Middlesex County — 2013–2019** 
6.	**Household Income by Race — 2013–2019** 
7.	**Poverty — 2019** 
8.	**Education — 2019** 
9.	**Employment Opportunities — 2010–2021**

## Key Findings
**Population and Age**

The age analysis showed that the **65-and-over category had the largest displayed population total** in the 2010–2019 data, at approximately **13.3 million**.

**Racial Demographics**

The statewide analysis showed **White Alone** as the largest racial category in the aggregated 2013–2019 data.

County-level analysis revealed differences between the three counties:
-	**Union County**: White Alone was the largest category, followed by Black or African American Alone. 
-	**Essex County**: White Alone and Black or African American Alone were much closer in size. 
-	**Middlesex County**: White Alone was the largest category, while **Asian Alone was the second-largest category**, at approximately 1.41 million. 

**Household Income**

The household-income analysis showed differences in **aggregated household income** across racial categories. Asian was the highest displayed category at approximately **$15 million**, followed by White Non-Hispanic at approximately **$13 million**.

**Poverty**

The 2019 poverty analysis showed differences in poverty rates across racial categories.

The displayed rates ranged from approximately:
-	**6% — White** 
-	**7% — Asian** 
-	**13% — Multiple** 
-	**17% — Black** 
-	**18% — Hispanic** 
-	**21% — Other** 
-	**25% — Islander** 

The analysis demonstrates the importance of examining **poverty rates in addition to the number of people experiencing poverty**, since population size differs between groups.

**Education**

The 2019 education analysis compared populations with **high school diplomas** and **bachelor's degrees** across racial categories.

White had the largest displayed totals for both measures. The Asian category showed a relatively smaller difference between its high school and bachelor's degree totals compared with several other larger racial categories.

**Employment**

The employment analysis examined industry employment levels and growth from **2010–2021**.
The highest displayed employment-growth values were:
-	**Professional & Business Services — 256** 
-	**Mining, Logging & Construction — 201** 
-	**Education & Health Services — 199** 

The dashboard also showed negative growth values for **Other Services, Government, and Information**.
